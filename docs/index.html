Track & Rules

Track

There are 2 options: 5km and 10km.

5km: to checkpoint 1 (Borishki zaliv) and back.

10km: closed loop

Rules

  • Every swimmer or group of swimmers should be supported by a kayak, SUP, or boat, and must carry a swimming buoy.
  • Feeding is self-supported: your kayaker should carry all you need for the swim.
  • Fins and hand paddles ARE allowed, but non-assisted swimmers are ranked first!
  • Wetsuits are allowed.
  • In case of emergency or to abort the swim, rely on your support kayaker/boat or call for a motorboat.

Base camp

BulSailing academy. (A few) kayaks and sailboats can be rented on the spot.
